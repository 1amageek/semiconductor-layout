# LayoutAutoGen

## Purpose and Scope

This Swift module owns layout generation, placement and routing algorithms.
Parent: [semiconductor-layout](../../DESIGN.md). The gate-contact generation
contract is owned by the [MOSFETGeneration child](MOSFETGeneration/DESIGN.md).
Other existing algorithms retain their current library contracts.

## Responsibilities and Boundaries

Generate layout IR from explicit caller parameters and technology rules. Consume
LayoutCore, LayoutTech and LayoutVerify public contracts. This module does not
own numerical PEX laws, characterization coefficients or artifact publication.

## Related Designs

| Design | Relationship | Contract Used | Summary | Cautions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Package](../../DESIGN.md) | parent | Public module composition | Placement and generation library | Source movement does not change products |
| [MOSFETGeneration](MOSFETGeneration/DESIGN.md) | child | Rule-admitted MOSFET geometry | Gate landings and device geometry | Preserve channel dimensions and terminal identity |
| [LayoutVerify](../LayoutVerify/DESIGN.md) | depends on | Device capture and extraction | Independent geometry and connectivity evidence | Positive contact alone does not prove enclosure |

## Architecture

```text
caller parameters + LayoutTech rules -> generation/placement/routing -> LayoutCore IR
                              MOSFETGeneration -> explicit gate contact landings
```

## Contracts and Invariants

Public generators return geometry or typed generation failures. They do not
substitute technology rules or numerical laws to obtain a successful result.
The MOSFET child owns its exact gate-contact geometry and device-preservation
guarantees; this module composes that public generator without duplicating them.

## State, Ownership, and Lifecycle

The MOSFET generator is a Sendable value; generated geometry belongs to the caller.
This change introduces no shared mutable state or asynchronous resource.

## Failure, Concurrency, and Constraints

Parameter, technology and placement failures propagate through AutoGenError.
The existing bounded finger-count admission remains in the parameter owner.

## Verification and Change Impact

[MOSFETGenerationTests](../../Tests/MOSFETGenerationTests/DESIGN.md) independently
checks geometry, device interpretation and standard-format roundtrip. Consumers
regenerate their master baseline and retain per-run content digests after a
generator change; prior generated bytes do not qualify the revised generator.
